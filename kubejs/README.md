# KubeJS: Remnant's custom systems

Hand-written: `startup_scripts/`, `server_scripts/`, `client_scripts/` and `assets/remnant/lang/`.
Generated by `tools/pack/build.py` (don't edit): `assets/remnant/textures/`, `assets/numismatics/` and `data/`.

Server scripts share one scope, so their top-level names must be unique across files. KubeJS 7 and Rhino rules learned the hard way:

- Server scripts can't assign to `global`.
- `const` inside a top-level loop or a `try` block is rejected ("redeclaration of var"), so move the body into a function.
- Don't redeclare KubeJS's globals (`AABB`, `BlockPos`, `Item`, `NBT`...) when loading a class; give it another name.
- Where KubeJS adds a getter with the same name as a vanilla method, the getter wins: `level.players`, `level.dimension`, `player.level` are properties, not calls. Some vanilla getters are hidden altogether (`level.getGameTime()`, `entity.getYRot()`); use `level.levelData.gameTime`, and `tp` to move a player while keeping their facing.
- `event.cancel()` ends the handler on the spot, so send messages first and cancel last.
- `BlockEvents.rightClicked` is skipped while the held item is on cooldown. Use `NativeEvents.onEvent` with NeoForge's `RightClickBlock` when a rule must hold whatever the player is holding.
- An error inside an item's `use`/`finishUsing` callback kicks the player, so catch it.

## The Ages (`server_scripts/ages.js`)

Five Ages, each an AStages **server-scope** stage (`age_2` to `age_5`), so an Age opens for everyone at once, including players who are offline or join later.

| Age | Opens when | World Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I. Embers | the start | Haven |
| II. Hearths | the first winter ends (Serene Seasons, checked every minute) | Frontier |
| III. Steam | someone uses a **Gearwright Schematic** | Ascent |
| IV. Industry | someone uses a **Foundry Codex** | Summit |
| V. Reclamation | someone uses an **Aether Core** | Pinnacle |

- **Keys out of order:** a key used before its Age says the pages make no sense yet, and isn't consumed.
- **Opening an Age:**
  - shows a title to everyone ("AGE III / STEAM") and a chat line naming who opened it
  - raises every player's Apotheosis World Tier (manual tier changes are off in `config/apotheosis`)
  - sets the `#age` score the city's restoration functions read, and runs `remnant:core/age<n>` if the city datapack is installed
- **Locks per Age:** the `LOCKS` table lists the mods and items each Age unlocks. Until then they can't be used, placed, mined, equipped or crafted, and they're hidden in EMI. They can still be picked up and stored, so a find isn't lost. Holding one shows "Age II: Hearths. Not yet."
- **Admin commands:**
  - `/remnant age` shows the current Age.
  - `/remnant age open <n>` opens the next Age.
  - `/remnant age reset <n>` sets the Age directly (testing only).

**Where the keys turn up** (`datapacks/remnant_ages`, NeoForge loot modifiers; Lootr rolls per player):

| Find | Chance per chest | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gearwright Schematic | 5% | mid ruins: mineshafts, dungeons, desert and jungle temples, stronghold libraries, Lost Cities, engineers' and mechanics' houses |
| Foundry Codex | 6% | deep ruins: ancient cities, bastion treasure, grand libraries, fortress keeps, Alex's Caves magnetic and toxic ruins |
| Aether Core | 10% | realms and boss vaults: end cities, the Aether's gold dungeon, the Otherside's temple apex, Cataclysm and BOMD arenas |
| Salvaged Circuitry | 20% | most ruins (for Relay Kits) |

Measured in game over 300 rolls: Gearwright 11 per 300 simple dungeons, Foundry Codex 23 and Aether Core 30 per 300 ancient cities.

## The stone-age start (`server_scripts/stone_age.js`)

1. Break leaves for sticks (an extra 20% chance on top of vanilla).
2. Dig gravel for flint (an extra 15% chance).
3. Right-click stone with flint to knap it into 1 or 2 **Flint Shards**.
4. Two shards and a stick make a **Flint Hatchet**, the only way to fell a tree: logs can't be broken without an axe.
5. Three shards and two sticks make a stone pickaxe, the flint pick.

There are no wooden tools at all.

## Getting around (`startup_scripts/hearthstone.js`, `server_scripts/travel.js`)

**Hearthstone.** Everyone gets one on their first login; more are crafted (flint shards and cobblestone around a campfire).
- **Bind:** sneak and right-click a lit campfire or a Cold Sweat hearth (a plain right-click works on a campfire). The stone's tooltip shows where it's bound. The binding lives on the stone, so a stone handed to a friend takes them to your fire.
- **Use:** hold right-click for ten seconds, from anywhere, any dimension. You land beside the hearth.
- **Cooldown:** one real-time hour, kept across relogs and deaths.
- **Interruptions:** taking damage breaks the channel. If the fire has been put out or broken, the stone says so and nothing is spent.
- **Admin:** `/remnant hearth reset <players>` clears the cooldown.

**Waystones.** Only the old world's waystones exist: every Waystones recipe is removed (waystones, sharestones, portstones, warp plates, scrolls, warp stones).
- **Unbreakable** outside creative.
- **Dark until woken.** Every waystone starts dark. A **Relay Kit** (Age II: ender pearls, amethyst, gold, stone bricks and a Salvaged Circuitry) wakes it, and the noise draws a wave of monsters: 2, plus 2 per player within 32 blocks, plus 1 per Age past II, up to 14. Witches and strays join from Age III, vindicators and pillagers from Age IV.
- **Lit for the whole server** once the wave is dead (or ten minutes after it began, if a monster wandered off). Each player still activates a waystone once, as usual.
- **Inventory warp button** on (`config/waystones-common.toml`), with the mod's own XP costs and five-minute button cooldown.
- **Creative placement:** waystones placed in creative start lit (for Remainder's own).
- **Admin:** `/remnant waystone wake` lights the waystone you're looking at.

## Guns from day one (`server_scripts/guns.js`)

The Create: Gunsmithing flintlock is hand-crafted in Age I (iron ingots, flint and steel, a stripped log) instead of needing Create's plates. Its paper cartridges need lead, gunpowder and paper. Later guns are locked by Age in `ages.js`, and every projectile's range is capped at 160 blocks in `datapacks/remnant_guns`.

## Tested

On a slim server (KubeJS, AStages, Create, Apotheosis, Serene Seasons, Farmer's Delight, Gunsmithing) with a headless client driven by xdotool:

- A bare hand can't break a log, and a hatchet can.
- Knapping 3 flint gave 4 shards.
- A Create cogwheel won't place in Age I and places in Age II.
- A schematic is refused in Age I and opens Age III from Age II: title shown, item consumed, World Tier raised to Ascent.
- Winter ending opens Age II.
- Hearthstone (with Waystones added to the slim rig):
  - the first login gives one
  - right-clicking a campfire binds it
  - a 10-second channel from 60 blocks away lands beside the fire
  - the cooldown survives a relog
  - damage breaks the channel
- Waystones:
  - a survival-placed waystone is dark, with an empty hand and with a recharging Hearthstone
  - a Relay Kit is refused in Age I
  - in Age II the kit draws a wave of 4 for one player, the waystone says it's waking, and it lights 2 seconds after the wave dies; then the normal Waystones menu opens
  - a stone pickaxe can't break it
  - the inventory warp button shows
- The hand-made flintlock, Hearthstone and Relay Kit recipes exist, and Waystones' own are gone.

All of it also loads cleanly on the full 262-mod server.
