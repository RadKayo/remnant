# KubeJS: Remnant's custom systems

Hand-written: `startup_scripts/`, `server_scripts/`, `client_scripts/` and `assets/remnant/lang/`.
Generated by `tools/pack/build.py` (don't edit): `assets/remnant/textures/`, `assets/numismatics/` and `data/`.

Server scripts share one scope, so their top-level names must be unique across files. Two KubeJS 7 and Rhino rules apply:

- Server scripts can't assign to `global`.
- `const` inside a top-level loop is rejected, so use a function.

## The Ages (`server_scripts/ages.js`)

Five Ages, each an AStages **server-scope** stage (`age_2` to `age_5`), so an Age opens for everyone at once, including players who are offline or join later.

| Age | Opens when | World Tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I. Embers | the start | Haven |
| II. Hearths | the first winter ends (Serene Seasons, checked every minute) | Frontier |
| III. Steam | someone uses a **Gearwright Schematic** | Ascent |
| IV. Industry | someone uses a **Foundry Codex** | Summit |
| V. Reclamation | someone uses an **Aether Core** | Pinnacle |

- **Keys out of order:** a key used before its Age says the pages make no sense yet, and isn't consumed.
- **Opening an Age:**
  - shows a title to everyone ("AGE III / STEAM") and a chat line naming who opened it
  - raises every player's Apotheosis World Tier (manual tier changes are off in `config/apotheosis`)
  - sets the `#age` score the city's restoration functions read, and runs `remnant:core/age<n>` if the city datapack is installed
- **Locks per Age:** the `LOCKS` table lists the mods and items each Age unlocks. Until then they can't be used, placed, mined, equipped or crafted, and they're hidden in EMI. They can still be picked up and stored, so a find isn't lost. Holding one shows "Age II: Hearths. Not yet."
- **Admin commands:**
  - `/remnant age` shows the current Age.
  - `/remnant age open <n>` opens the next Age.
  - `/remnant age reset <n>` sets the Age directly (testing only).

**Where the keys turn up** (`datapacks/remnant_ages`, NeoForge loot modifiers; Lootr rolls per player):

| Find | Chance per chest | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gearwright Schematic | 5% | mid ruins: mineshafts, dungeons, desert and jungle temples, stronghold libraries, Lost Cities, engineers' and mechanics' houses |
| Foundry Codex | 6% | deep ruins: ancient cities, bastion treasure, grand libraries, fortress keeps, Alex's Caves magnetic and toxic ruins |
| Aether Core | 10% | realms and boss vaults: end cities, the Aether's gold dungeon, the Otherside's temple apex, Cataclysm and BOMD arenas |
| Salvaged Circuitry | 20% | most ruins (for Relay Kits) |

Measured in game over 300 rolls: Gearwright 11 per 300 simple dungeons, Foundry Codex 23 and Aether Core 30 per 300 ancient cities.

## The stone-age start (`server_scripts/stone_age.js`)

1. Break leaves for sticks (an extra 20% chance on top of vanilla).
2. Dig gravel for flint (an extra 15% chance).
3. Right-click stone with flint to knap it into 1 or 2 **Flint Shards**.
4. Two shards and a stick make a **Flint Hatchet**, the only way to fell a tree: logs can't be broken without an axe.
5. Three shards and two sticks make a stone pickaxe, the flint pick.

There are no wooden tools at all.

## Tested

On a slim server (KubeJS, AStages, Create, Apotheosis, Serene Seasons, Farmer's Delight, Gunsmithing) with a headless client driven by xdotool:

- A bare hand can't break a log, and a hatchet can.
- Knapping 3 flint gave 4 shards.
- A Create cogwheel won't place in Age I and places in Age II.
- A schematic is refused in Age I and opens Age III from Age II: title shown, item consumed, World Tier raised to Ascent.
- Winter ending opens Age II.

All of it also loads cleanly on the full 262-mod server.
